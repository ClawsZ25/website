My Game Development Journey

Picnic Pocketers

A fun game about colleting food

Picnic Pocketers is a game I helped develop in Unreal Engine 4.7 as a part of a software engineering course. It is small game about collecting as much food as possible in the given time while avoiding different mobs and navigating different worlds.

Download (Windows)

AATTMF

Accident at the Time Machine Factory

AATTMF is a game I helped develop in Unreal Engine 5.3.2 as a part of a software engineering course. After a Time machine blows up at the factory players, traverse across 4 time periods: Prehistoric, Medieval, Western, and Future. They have collect parts to put the Time machine back together, reknit the time-stream, and save the world.

Download (Windows)

Contract Construction

Infiltrate and complete the mission

Contract Construction is the first 3D game project I helped develop. It is a spy-like game where the player must avoid detection, collect the right keys, and defeat the Ultimate Bossman.

Download (Windows)

Mitten's Great Adventure

Recover the Whole(y) Milk Jug

Mitten's Great Adventure was my first venture in to 2D Game Development and Game Development as a whole. It's a short and sweet platformer where you must collect coins, navigate the world, and get to the milk.

Yeti Run

Ski down the mountain, and stop the Lava People

This is the result of my second Game Jam that I did with a bunch of buddies. The theme was Momentum and the extra themes we applied were freeze and duplication. It was a fun figuring out how to make a line drawing game. We placed in the middle of the pack.
